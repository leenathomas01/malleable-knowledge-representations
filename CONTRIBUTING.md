# Contributing

We welcome disconfirming evidence, counterexamples, clear experimental designs, replication attempts, and precise links to related work.

- Distinguish direct evidence from analogy, inference, and speculation.
- Avoid grand novelty or AGI claims without strong evidence.
- For empirical work, supply conditions, baselines, data generation/splits, seeds, compute/verification costs, uncertainty, and failure cases.
- Compare against competent adaptive libraries, not intentionally weak fixed caches.
- For latent-space claims, demonstrate downstream behavior; embedding geometry alone is insufficient.
- Keep broad conceptual revisions separate from the narrow callable `reshape()` protocol.
- Do not disclose private data, credentials, proprietary weights, or unsafe deployment instructions.
- Disclose substantial AI assistance and verify all AI-supplied citations.

A result showing that a proposed mechanism is redundant or ineffective is a valuable contribution.
